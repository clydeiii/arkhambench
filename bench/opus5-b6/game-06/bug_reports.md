## 2026-07-25T09:07:35+00:00
- Round: 10
- Phase: Investigation
- Description: MISSING SAFETY PROMPT for attack-of-opportunity actions. docs_agent/playing_guide.md states: 'The engine asks for confirmation before likely-mistake actions: investigating a location with 0 clues, or taking an action that will provoke attacks of opportunity.' In this game the engine never asked for such a confirmation. R10, Ghoul Pits: I was engaged with a ready Corpse-Hungry Ghoul at 3/5 horror and chose 'Take resource (gain 1)'. The engine executed it immediately with no confirmation; the ghoul's attack of opportunity dealt 2 damage + 2 horror and defeated me. Same thing at R5 when I chose 'Move to Bedroom' while engaged with Swarm of Rats — no confirmation, the AoO just resolved (I did get a Dodge window there, but that is the cancel window, not the documented confirmation). The RULES adjudication looks correct to me: taking a resource is an action other than fight/evade/parley/resign so it provokes, and per the AoO rule the attack resolves after costs are paid but before the action's effect, so the resource was not yet available to pay for Dodge. So this is a documented-behavior/UI gap rather than a rules error — but it is the exact safety rail the guide promises, and its absence decided the game.
